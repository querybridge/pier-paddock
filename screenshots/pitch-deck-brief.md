# Pier & Paddock — Merchant Pitch Brief

Source material for generating a **merchant-facing pitch deck**. The audience is a
watch **supplier/merchant** (e.g. a dropship inventory partner) deciding whether to
list their stock on Pier & Paddock. Pair this brief with the nine screenshots in
this folder.

> **Note:** Pier & Paddock is a demonstration storefront. All figures, members and
> sales shown are illustrative demo data — use them to convey scale and mechanics,
> not as real performance numbers.

---

## The one-liner

**Pier & Paddock is a curated luxury-watch marketplace that presents your
inventory beautifully, sells it through an account-based loyalty program that turns
buyers into repeat collectors, and hands you live sales data and real demand signals
— so you move more pieces at strong prices with less work.**

---

## Who it's for

The **merchant** is the supply side: a retailer/distributor (the demo uses a generic
stand-in, "Meridian Watch Supply") who lists watches on Pier & Paddock. Pier &
Paddock owns the storefront, the customer relationship, the loyalty program and
fulfilment; the merchant supplies inventory and watches their sales roll in.

---

## What the platform does (site functions)

1. **Premium storefront** — a polished, mobile-first shopping experience: animated
   home, faceted shop, fast filtering, and rich product pages built for high
   consideration purchases. (Screens 01–04.)
2. **Account-based, secure checkout** — given the size of these transactions,
   purchasing requires an account (no anonymous guest checkout). Shoppers log in or
   register at a clean gateway, which also seeds the loyalty program.
3. **The Crest Membership (loyalty engine)** — a 1–5 "crest" program driven by
   lifetime spend (Member → Patron → Collector → Curator → Steward). It rewards
   repeat buying with early access, price tracking, store credit, a concierge and
   private "first-look" inventory — converting one-time buyers into recurring,
   higher-spending collectors. (Screen 06.)
4. **The Vault** — every shopper's personal collection tracker: saved watches,
   live secondary-market values and a portfolio total. It reframes a watch as an
   *investment*, giving customers a reason to come back — and buy again.
   (Screen 08.)
4b. **The Grail List** — members register the exact pieces they're hunting (brand,
   model, reference, target price). This captures forward buying intent and feeds
   the merchant-facing grail-demand signal. (Screen 09.)
5. **Merchant Portal** — the merchant's own back office: live sales, revenue, top
   sellers, listings, and an XML feed to keep inventory in sync. (Screen 05.)
6. **Operator Console & demand intelligence** — program analytics including
   **grail demand**: the aggregated list of exact references collectors are hunting
   — a direct "stock this and it will sell" signal for merchants. (Screen 07.)
7. **Trust & fulfilment** — every watch independently authenticated, insured
   signature-required delivery, box-and-papers verification.

---

## Why a merchant wins (value props)

- **Better presentation → better conversion.** Your watches are shown like the
  assets they are, at luxury price points, on every device.
- **Onboarding is trivial.** Add a listing by hand, or point us at your **XML feed**
  and we keep listings, prices and stock in sync.
- **Demand is engineered, not hoped for.** The loyalty program + Vault drive repeat,
  higher-value purchases — you sell *more to the same buyers over time*.
- **You see your numbers in real time.** Sales this month, lifetime revenue, top
  sellers — your dashboard, scoped to only your inventory.
- **We tell you what to stock.** Grail-demand data turns guesswork into a buy list.
- **Zero storefront overhead.** No site to build, no checkout to maintain, no
  marketing engine to run — you supply watches; we sell them.

## The screenshots — what each shows and how to use it

### `01-home.png` — Home page
The storefront's first impression: an animated hero ("Haute Horology, Within
Reach"), featured collections, and a New Arrivals row of product cards.
- **Talking point:** This is the shop window your inventory lives in — editorial,
  unhurried, built to sell five- and six-figure watches.
- **Deck use:** Opening "the experience" slide.

### `02-shop-all.png` — Shop (All Watches)
The full catalogue with the faceted sidebar (brand, collection, price, case
material, condition) and a clean product grid.
- **Talking point:** Buyers find pieces fast; every card shows brand, model,
  reference, price and key specs at a glance.
- **Deck use:** "Discovery / merchandising" slide.

### `03-plp-rolex.png` — Product listing, filtered (Rolex)
A brand-filtered listing with removable active-filter chips and result count.
- **Talking point:** Stackable filters + chips = frictionless browsing; your stock
  surfaces exactly when a buyer narrows in.
- **Deck use:** Pair with 02 to show shoppability, or use instead of it.

### `04-pdp.png` — Product detail page (Rolex Cosmograph Daytona)
A full product page: sharp gallery, an at-a-glance key-spec grid (case size,
movement, material, water resistance), Add to Cart / Save to Vault / Compare, the
full specification table and description.
- **Talking point:** This is how *your* watch is presented — complete specs,
  authenticated, insured delivery messaging, and a one-tap path to purchase or save.
- **Deck use:** "How your product looks to a buyer" — a strong, concrete slide.

### `05-merchant-console.png` — Merchant Portal (★ the merchant's view)
The merchant's own dashboard ("Meridian Watch Supply"): revenue this month and
lifetime, units sold, top-selling pieces, active listing count, and XML-feed sync
status.
- **Talking point:** Your back office. Real-time sales and revenue, your best
  sellers, and inventory kept in sync via feed — scoped to only your stock.
- **Deck use:** **The hero merchant slide.** "Here's what you'd log into."

### `06-membership.png` — Crest Membership (the demand engine)
A member's dashboard at Collector tier: the crest mark, progress to the next tier,
the full benefit ladder (unlocked + locked teasers), Retailer's Credit balance, a
concierge block and the quarterly market report.
- **Talking point:** This is *why buyers come back*. Tiers, store credit, early
  access and price tracking convert a one-time sale into a lifetime collector — more
  repeat demand for your inventory.
- **Deck use:** "The loyalty flywheel" slide — connect it to merchant revenue.

### `07-operator-console.png` — Operator Console (demand intelligence)
Program analytics: members by tier, total Retailer's Credit liability, engagement
(Vault saves, active grails, opt-ins), and **Grail Demand** — the most-requested
brands/models/references.
- **Talking point:** "The signal we feed the retailer." We aggregate exactly which
  references collectors are hunting — so you stock what's already in demand.
- **Deck use:** "Demand intelligence / what to stock" slide — a differentiator.

### `08-vault.png` — The Vault (investment tracking)
A Collector's Vault: a portfolio summary (total invested, current value, total
change %), an **Owned** table showing purchase price, latest secondary value and
gain with sparklines, and a **Watching** list with one-tap Add to Cart.
- **Talking point:** Customers track their watches like an investment portfolio.
  Seeing gains turns a purchase into a reason to buy *again* — and live price
  tracking unlocks at Collector tier, pulling buyers further up the ladder.
- **Deck use:** Pair with `06` on the "flywheel / why buyers return" slide.

### `09-grail-list.png` — The Grail List (captured demand)
A member's grail list: the specific pieces they're hunting — brand, model,
reference and target price — with live Watching/Matched status, plus an add form.
- **Talking point:** Buyers tell us exactly what they want to acquire next. That
  intent rolls up into the grail-demand signal (Screen 07) — a name-and-reference
  buy list handed to merchants.
- **Deck use:** Bridge slide between the loyalty story (`06`/`08`) and demand
  intelligence (`07`).

## Suggested deck flow

1. **Title** — Pier & Paddock × [Merchant]. "Sell more, present better, work less."
2. **The opportunity** — luxury watch buyers want curation, trust and an experience.
3. **The experience** — `01-home` (+ `02`/`03`).
4. **How your product looks to a buyer** — `04-pdp`.
5. **Your back office** — `05-merchant-console` (revenue, top sellers, feed).
6. **The flywheel: loyalty drives repeat demand** — `06-membership` + `08-vault`
   (the investment hook that brings buyers back).
7. **Buyers tell us what they want** — `09-grail-list` (captured intent) →
   `07-operator-console` grail demand: "we tell you what to stock."
8. **How it works** — list manually or via XML feed; we present, sell, fulfil.
9. **Trust** — authentication, insured signature delivery, box & papers.
10. **The ask / next steps** — connect your feed; go live.

## Brand & tone notes (for slide design)

- **Palette:** deep navy `#1B2A41` and muted gold `#9A7B43` on warm off-white;
  understated, editorial, restrained — nautical + motorsport heritage, never flashy.
- **Type feel:** serif display headings (Georgia-style) over clean sans body.
- **Voice:** confident, quiet luxury. Sell provenance, presentation and demand —
  not discounts.
- **Crest motif:** a minimal monogram shield ("P&P"), rendered 1–5 times for tier —
  reuse it as a tasteful deck accent.
