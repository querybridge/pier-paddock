    Are your watches authentic?

    Yes. Every timepiece is independently authenticated before sale and again prior to dispatch. We never list replicas or watches of uncertain origin.

    How is my watch shipped and insured?

    Orders are shipped overnight, signature-required, and fully insured for their complete value while in transit. Complimentary insured overnight shipping is included; Saturday delivery is available for a small surcharge.

    What is your returns policy?

    Because each watch is a unique, authenticated piece, returns are handled case-by-case. Contact our concierge within 48 hours of delivery and we will guide you through the insured return process.

    Do you offer sizing and servicing?

    Yes. Our workshop can size bracelets and advise on servicing. Sizing requests can be noted at checkout or arranged with our concierge after purchase.

    What does “Box & Papers” mean?

    “Full Set” means the watch comes with its original box, warranty card and booklets. “Watch Only” means those accessories are not included. Each listing states its status clearly.
